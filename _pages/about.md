As a postdoctoral fellow at the [Whole Person & Population Modelling Lab](https://www.felskylab.com/), I lead the predictive modelling efforts for an [NIH-funded grant](https://reporter.nih.gov/search/Ot-rVrV750CHAHTHP7az_A/project-details/10954667) investigating treatment-resistant late-life depression. I also work with biobank-scale datasets to develop biopsychosocial machine learning models of suicide-related phenotypes.

My research focuses on understanding psychopathology in relation to suicide and sociodemographic factors, particularly among transitional-aged youth. I am passionate about advancing this work and am actively seeking postdoctoral opportunities where I can continue conducting impactful, interdisciplinary research.
